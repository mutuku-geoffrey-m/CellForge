# References and Authoritative Literature

## 1. Manufacturing Systems & Additive Manufacturing

1. **Gibson, I., Rosen, D., Stucker, B., & Khorasani, M.** (2021). *Additive Manufacturing Technologies* (3rd ed.). Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-56127-7
   - Primary reference for Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF) build envelope utilization, support structure generation, powder recycling loss ratios, and thermal stress-relief cycles.
2. **Groover, M. P.** (2020). *Fundamentals of Modern Manufacturing: Materials, Processes, and Systems* (7th ed.). John Wiley & Sons.
   - Foundational reference for conventional subtractive machining, 3-axis CNC vertical milling cycle-time modeling, and chip removal volume fractions for 6061-T6 aluminum.
3. **Kalpakjian, S., & Schmid, S. R.** (2014). *Manufacturing Engineering and Technology* (7th ed.). Pearson.
   - Reference for surface finishing, vibratory deburring mechanisms, and cutting tool life considerations.

---

## 2. Operations Research, Queuing Dynamics & Factory Physics

4. **Hopp, W. J., & Spearman, M. L.** (2011). *Factory Physics* (3rd ed.). Waveland Press.
   - Theoretical foundation for Little's Law ($\text{WIP} = \text{TH} \times \text{CT}$), Kingman's heavy-traffic approximations, variability pooling, and bottleneck definition under stochastic flow.
5. **Kingman, J. F. C.** (1961). *The Single Server Queue in Heavy Traffic*. Mathematical Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society, 57(4), 902–904.
   - Derivation of the heavy-traffic approximation for queue waiting time as a function of server utilization and arrival/service coefficients of variation.
6. **Little, J. D. C.** (1961). *A Proof for the Queuing Formula: $L = \lambda W$*. Operations Research, 9(3), 383–387.
   - Formal mathematical derivation of the work-in-progress, flow rate, and lead time relationship.
7. **Hillier, F. S., & Lieberman, G. J.** (2021). *Introduction to Operations Research* (11th ed.). McGraw-Hill Education.
   - Linear programming and mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) branch-and-bound formulation techniques for resource allocation and capacity constraints.

---

## 3. Maintenance Engineering & Productivity Metrics

8. **Nakajima, S.** (1988). *Introduction to TPM: Total Productive Maintenance*. Productivity Press.
   - Classical formulation of Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) and the decomposition into Availability, Performance, and Quality efficiency ratios.
9. **Ebeling, C. E.** (2019). *An Introduction to Reliability and Maintainability Engineering* (3rd ed.). Waveland Press.
   - Mathematical definitions for Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF), Mean Time To Repair (MTTR), and availability calculations under exponential and lognormal failure distributions.

---

## 4. Quality Control & Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

10. **ASTM International.** (2021). *ASTM E1417 / E1417M-21: Standard Practice for Liquid Penetrant Testing*. ASTM International, West Conshohocken, PA. https://doi.org/10.1520/E1417_E1417M-21
    - Technical baseline for penetrant application, emulsification, developer dwell times, and ultraviolet illumination requirements for fluorescent liquid penetrant inspection.
11. **International Organization for Standardization.** (2021). *ISO 3452-1:2021: Non-destructive testing — Penetrant testing — Part 1: General principles*. ISO, Geneva, Switzerland.
    - Global reference standard for surface-breaking defect detection in metallic components.
12. **Montgomery, D. C.** (2020). *Introduction to Statistical Quality Control* (8th ed.). John Wiley & Sons.
    - Reference for First-Pass Yield (FPY), rework loops, scrap accounting, and Type I (false positive) vs Type II (quality escape) classification error risks.
