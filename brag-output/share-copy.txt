CellForge couples discrete-event simulation (SimPy) with Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (SciPy MILP) to answer a core manufacturing question: conventional CNC machining or metal 3D printing? Evaluated across a 160-hour benchmark campaign, the optimizer reduced unit costs by 20%, cut production lead time by 70%, and saved $95,000 in equipment CAPEX. Open-source, verified with 22 automated stress tests, and 100% reproducible.
